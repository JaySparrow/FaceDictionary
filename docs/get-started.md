# Get Started with FaceDictionary

FaceDictionary finds people in your own photo library by their face. It runs entirely on your Mac: everything is kept in an encrypted database, and the app has no network access.

## Install

1. Open `FaceDictionary-1.1.0.dmg` and drag **FaceDictionary** onto **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. If macOS says it cannot check the app, go to *System Settings → Privacy & Security* and click **Open Anyway** once.

## First start

1. Choose a password of at least 8 characters for your new database. There is no way in without it.
2. Set three security questions. Answering two of them lets you reset a forgotten password.

Your database is kept in `~/Library/Application Support/FaceDictionary/`.

## Build your library

- **Import a folder:** in **Library**, click the import button and choose a folder with one sub-folder of photos per person. The sub-folder names become the names. Your files are only read, never changed.
- **Or add people one by one:** **+** (⌘N) creates an entry. Add photos with the **+** above the album, ⌘O, or by dragging them in.

## Search

In **Search**, drop a photo, paste one (⌘V) or open one (⌘O). Every face is compared with your library and the five closest entries are shown with a score. In a group photo, click a face to see its matches. **✓ This is the person** adds the photo to that entry. **History** keeps every search.

## Good to know

- **Edit:** double-click any text to change it. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
- **Fields:** right-click beside the fields to add one.
- **Undo:** ⌘Z undoes any change in the Library, step by step.
- **Select several:** ⌘-click or ⇧-click, then ⌫ deletes them all.
- **Deleting:** deleted items go to the **Trash** until you remove them there.
- **Lock:** ⌘L locks the app. It also locks itself after a few idle minutes.
- **Settings** (bottom of the sidebar): light or dark appearance, recognition model, auto-lock time, password and recovery options.
